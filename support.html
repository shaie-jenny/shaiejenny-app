Common questions

Does my partner see my saved dates and history?
Not yet. Everything stays on the phone it was saved on, so each of you has your own. Use "Send to Your Partner" to share a date.

How do I hide dates with alcohol?
Open "More options" under the filters and turn on No alcohol.

How do I stop seeing a date?
Open it and tap "Not for us? Hide this date". You can bring it back from the bottom of Date History.

How do I add the widget?
Press and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Date of the Day from the list. Open the app once first so the widget has today's date.

Will I lose everything if I delete the app?
Yes. Your dates and history are only stored on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them too.
