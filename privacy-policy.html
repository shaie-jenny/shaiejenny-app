Privacy Policy

ShaieJenny (the app) · Last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

The app doesn't collect any of your data. There's no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you put into the app stays on your phone.

What's stored, and where

Your profile answers, saved dates, date history, ratings and settings are stored only on your device. They're never sent to us or anyone else. The Home Screen widget reads them from the same place on your phone.

If you delete the app, all of this is deleted with it.

Find Nearby

When you tap Find Nearby, the app opens Apple Maps with a search word (for example "pottery class"). Apple Maps uses your location to show places near you. The app itself never sees or stores your location. Apple's own privacy policy covers Apple Maps.

Sending a date to your partner

If you share a date, it goes through your phone's normal share sheet to whichever app you choose. The app doesn't see who you send it to.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if syncing between partners is added), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
